# Proton: Little Synths With BIG Sounds #2

Proton is a small synth with only one IC. It operates from 2 AA batteries. It is number 2 in my "Little synths with BIG sounds" series. You can connect it to a sequencer, or play it alone.

![Proton](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f3aa9fc1-f2ad-43b1-b5ff-cc4e480b8870)

## Build guide and video

- Step-by-step build guide on Instructables: [Proton - Little Synths With BIG Sounds #2](https://www.instructables.com/Proton-Little-Synths-With-BIG-Sounds-2/)
- Video on YouTube: [Proton](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Ci_B_42n48)

## About the design

The synth circuit is the [Totoro 1-IC simple synth](https://syntherjack.net/totoro-1-ic-simple-synth/) by Synther Jack. I made a new PCB and a front panel, and I added some changes. Synther Jack's page explains how the circuit works.

The build is easy. You need a soldering iron and basic soldering skills. There is no case and no complex power supply.

## What you need

- All parts are in the parts list ([PDF](Parts%20List/Proton%20-%20Part%20List%20-%20PDF.pdf) or Excel).
- The schematic is [Proton - Schematic.png](Proton%20-%20Schematic.png).
- The Gerber files for the PCB and the front panel are in [Gerber Files](Gerber%20Files/). You can send them to a PCB maker.
- 2 AA batteries

## Build it

The Instructables guide shows each step, with photos. In short:

1. Send the Gerber files to a PCB maker.
2. Get the parts in the parts list.
3. Solder the parts to the PCB.
4. Attach the front panel.
5. Connect the power and test the circuit.
